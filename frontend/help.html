Food Facts

Discover seasonal produce and simple ways to keep food fresh.

What's in season

Summer produce includes stone fruit, berries, zucchini, and tomatoes.

Keep it fresh

Store leafy greens with a dry paper towel to help absorb excess moisture.

Reduce waste

Use soft fruit in smoothies, sauces, or baking before it becomes spoiled.

What's in season

Autumn is ideal for apples, pears, pumpkin, and sweet potato.

Smart storage

Keep potatoes, onions, and garlic in a cool, dark, dry place.

What's in season

Winter brings citrus, broccoli, cauliflower, and root vegetables.
