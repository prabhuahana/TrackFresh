Help

How to get the most out of FreshTrack.

Adding food

Go to Inventory, enter the food name, expiry date, and quantity, then tap Add Item. Star ★ items you buy every week.

Expiry tracking

Your Home page shows a "Use First" section for items expiring within 3 days. Enable notifications in Profile to get browser reminders.

Shopping list

Add items manually or tap + Shop on any pantry item. Weekly staples from starred foods can be re-added with one tap.

Recipes

Recipes are matched to your pantry and filtered by your diet. Items expiring soon are prioritised. Tap "Add missing to list" to shop for ingredients.

Profile & diet

Set your diet type and food preferences in Profile. Recipe suggestions will respect these settings.

Receipt scanning

Paste a receipt in Scan and review the items before adding them. We never store card details, and raw receipt text is deleted after import.

Dark mode

Toggle dark/light mode using the button at the bottom of the sidebar. Your preference is saved automatically.
